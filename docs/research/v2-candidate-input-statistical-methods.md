# V2 CandidateInput statistical methods

This research supports corrective issue [#70](https://github.com/Awisalas/match-vet/issues/70). It defines defensible mathematical objects and separates them from decisions that need MatchVet evidence. It does not instantiate a policy, select numerical constants, fit a model, acquire history, or authorize production use.

The settled authority is [#13](https://github.com/Awisalas/match-vet/issues/13), especially Historical Baseline, Selection Strength, threshold learning and chronological evaluation. Existing contracts retain cutoff-bound [F13 inputs and predictions](../f13-v2-model-contract.md) and replayable [F14 decision bundles](../f14-v2-decision-contract.md). Research methods cannot supply missing historical cases or replace these identities.

## Chronological case supply

A calibration or evaluation case is an immutable prediction made without its outcome, joined later to that outcome. Retain prediction cutoff, input digest, feature/model/configuration identities, prediction distribution, training and calibration membership, fixture identity, outcome availability time and settlement version. Include a result only after its protected historical source was available at the current cutoff. A later correction must identify a new source revision. Missing publication or observation history cannot be reconstructed by assuming that a final downloaded table existed earlier.

Development uses nested rolling origins. At each origin, fitting, preprocessing, hyperparameter selection, baselines and calibration use preceding observations. Freeze a candidate before predicting the next untouched block. Policy selection uses later validation; final evaluation follows validation and remains untouched. Reusing a final evaluation to change the method makes it development history. This follows the forecast-origin principle, but the MatchVet batch cutoff and outcome-availability requirements are repository requirements. [Hyndman and Athanasopoulos, time series cross-validation](https://otexts.com/fpp3/tscv.html).

For calibration, retain raw predictions generated by an earlier compatible model and outcomes revealed later. The calibration fitter and its model input cannot contain the target result. Identity calibration is a candidate requiring evaluation, not proof that probabilities are calibrated. Flexible methods need stronger evidence; beta calibration includes the identity map, whereas ordinary logistic calibration can change an already calibrated predictor. A method's success on another dataset does not establish its validity here. [Kull, Silva Filho and Flach 2017](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v54/kull17a.html).

## Exact settlement scores and reliability

Let `K` be the preference's actual outcome set: WIN and LOSS for binary preferences; WIN, PUSH and LOSS when PUSH is possible. For retained case `i`, `p_ik` is its frozen settlement probability and `y_ik` is the indicator of its observed settlement. Define categorical Brier loss as `sum_k (p_ik - y_ik)^2` and log loss as `-log(p_i,observed)`. Zero probability assigned to the realized outcome has infinite log loss; silently adding an epsilon changes the rule. VOID supplies no predictive score. PUSH stays a separate class and never becomes WIN. These are negatively oriented proper scores. Compare model and baseline on identical eligible cases, using paired loss differences. [Gneiting and Raftery 2007, categorical scoring rules](https://sites.stat.washington.edu/people/raftery/Research/PDF/Gneiting2007jasa.pdf).

The empirical losses are defined once cases exist. A bounded historical reliability component `R`, its shrinkage and gate acceptance require an additional frozen mapping. Brier skill ratios are not automatically proper scores, and a new arbitrary `1 - error` mapping is not justified by the proper-score literature. Report score, comparison, interval and evidence status separately until a validated transformation exists. The source proves score properties; this restriction on `R` is an inference from those properties and #13's frozen transformation requirement.

Calibration examines the full settlement vector. For each predeclared group `g` and outcome `k`, retain observed frequency `sum_i y_ik / N_g`, mean predicted probability `sum_i p_ik / N_g`, their difference and a dependence-aware interval. Groups and bin boundaries must be chosen before their evaluation outcomes. Top-label accuracy or top-class calibration cannot prove WIN/LOSS/PUSH calibration. [Vaicenavicius et al. 2019](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v89/vaicenavicius19a.html).

## Hierarchical baseline candidate

One mathematically coherent candidate is a categorical Dirichlet model. For exact-settlement group `g`, retain unweighted counts `n_gk`, prior concentration parameters `a_gk > 0`, and `A_g = sum_k a_gk`. Multiplying the multinomial likelihood by the Dirichlet density gives posterior parameters `a_gk + n_gk`. The mean is `b_gk = (a_gk + n_gk)/(A_g + N_g)`. A marginal probability has Beta parameters `a_gk + n_gk` and `A_g + N_g - a_gk - n_gk`. This conjugate calculation follows directly from the documented density. It is a candidate estimator, not a selected MatchVet prior. [SciPy Dirichlet definition](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.stats.dirichlet.html).

Binary groups use a two-outcome Beta model with posterior parameters `a_WIN + n_WIN` and `a_LOSS + n_LOSS`; PUSH probability is structurally zero. If a frozen tail probability is `q`, an equal-tail posterior interval is the Beta quantile pair at `q/2` and `1-q/2`. Its validity is conditional on the prior and likelihood assumptions. These intervals are not automatically empirical frequentist guarantees under clustered or changing football data. [SciPy Beta definition and quantiles](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.stats.beta.html).

The repository fixes the borrowing order: family and exact line, then role, league, and supported context. It does not fix `a_gk`, the amount of pooling, regime handling, decay, specialization precision or confidence level. A possible parent-centered prior is `a_gk = lambda_g * b_parent,k`, but `lambda_g` and valid parent construction need development data. Parent and child counts cannot count the same observation twice as independent evidence. Fractional recency weights are a different likelihood/weighting decision and need their own validation. Broad unrelated preferences cannot supply an unknown root base rate. Fall back to a supported ancestor only under a frozen eligibility rule; if no valid ancestor exists, baseline and dependent lift remain UNKNOWN. An observed zero count is evidence about frequency, not proof of impossibility.

Minimum evidence means actual cutoff-valid settlements in each claimed pooling population, chronological predictive comparisons, uncertainty diagnostics and sufficient interval precision for the declared decision. Neither the conjugate formulas nor another football paper determines a minimum match count, concentration, triviality cutoff or discrimination margin for MatchVet. All remain INCONCLUSIVE until the development corpus and declared precision/tolerance objectives can support them.

## Dependence, precision and paired ties

Preference rows from one fixture share outcomes and inputs. Preserve all rows of a fixture together when estimating uncertainty. Shared teams, Matchweeks and nearby regimes can add temporal dependence. Block bootstrap research supports resampling dependent histories under stated assumptions, but does not grant validity for arbitrary nonstationarity. Block length is a statistical tuning decision; data-dependent selectors use estimated serial correlation, rather than a convenient fixed number. [Politis and White 2004, author manuscript](https://www.math.ucsd.edu/~politis/SBblock-revER.pdf) and [author publication list including correction](https://mathweb.ucsd.edu/~politis/DPpublication.html).

For candidates `a,b`, compute each replicate's difference `D_r = T15_strength(a; Z_r) - T15_strength(b; Z_r)` using the same resampled historical blocks and coherent shared parameter/calibration/scenario draw `Z_r`. Recompute the existing T15 transformations under the frozen policy; do not create a second ranking score. The variance identity `Var(S_a-S_b) = Var(S_a)+Var(S_b)-2 Cov(S_a,S_b)` explains why separate unpaired intervals are insufficient. Retain the complete joint difference distribution and its construction, not just candidate widths. This is an inference from dependent resampling and covariance algebra.

The settled tie rule uses a frozen paired difference interval containing zero. Its confidence level, percentile versus other interval construction, block specification, draw law, replication precision and supported pair/cohort definition still need validation. A current-candidate latent strength difference and a historical mean difference of realized performance are different estimands. Historical performance comparisons can validate ranking; they do not replace current-candidate difference uncertainty. Missing coherent draws or insufficient paired historical precision leaves tie status UNKNOWN. Arbitrary fixture preference order cannot assert statistical distinguishability.

Effective evidence is metric-specific. Retain unique fixture and Matchweek counts, correlation groups, weights and interval precision. `(sum w)^2 / sum w^2` measures weight concentration and cannot alone account for temporal or overlapping-market dependence. Minimum evidence should be derived from the frozen uncertainty/precision objective and verified on development folds. Thirty-seven rows for one fixture are not thirty-seven independent trials.

## G6 coverage and tail diagnostics

Three targets must remain distinct. Calibration asks whether outcomes agree with probabilities in populations of comparable predictions. A prediction region covers a future observed count or settlement. A probability confidence interval targets the unobserved conditional probability itself. Gupta, Podkopaev and Ramdas formally distinguish prediction-set coverage `P(Y in S(X))`, probability interval coverage `P(E[Y|X] in I(X))`, and calibration `E[Y|f(X)] = f(X)`. A single fixture outcome does not reveal its conditional WIN probability. Counting observed WIN indicators inside F13 probability intervals therefore measures none of the claimed latent probability coverage. [Gupta, Podkopaev and Ramdas 2020](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2020/file/26d88423fc6da243ffddf161ca712757-Paper.pdf).

G6 needs a frozen estimand and validation law for every coverage field. Model-based simulation with known conditional probabilities can examine parameter-interval behavior under declared assumptions. Later group-level settlement calibration and count-distribution tail checks assess real outcomes. Neither establishes distribution-free per-fixture latent probability coverage. Simulation conclusions must stay labeled model-conditional. If #13 intends literal empirical per-fixture probability coverage from one observed settlement, its measurable criterion must be clarified before implementation.

For a frozen measurable tail event `A` of actual goals/corners, retain `t_i = P_i(A)` and `z_i = 1{outcome_i in A}` and evaluate their calibration and proper-score behavior on later cases. Retain truncation/missing probability mass and sensitivity runs over declared model perturbations. Tail events, perturbation magnitudes, mass tolerance, coverage level and acceptance bands require explicit predeclaration and data. Count-tail evidence does not repair unavailable calibrated settlement uncertainty.

## Data Quality, research completeness and risk

Cutoff fidelity, protected provenance, known versus UNKNOWN requirements, coverage and attempted research states are directly observable. These support dimension evidence and completion records. They do not choose numeric dimension scores, freshness curves, aggregation weights, authority discounts or materiality penalties. The evidence-to-score mapping and criticality catalog must be frozen and evaluated independently of probability calibration. UNKNOWN cannot be converted to a zero-valued observed fact, a neutral source or a completed negative research finding.

The adversarial producer can preserve evidence-linked Supporting Case and Failure Case before vetting. It must state affected assumptions, exact evidence IDs, conflict resolution, whether a scenario already appears in model uncertainty, and proposed consequence. A credible fact that contradicts a required assumption can justify an explicit qualitative gate decision under a frozen rule. It cannot supply a scenario probability, numeric failure risk or strength penalty without a validated quantitative mechanism. Scenario effect estimates need cutoff-valid scenario inputs, a frozen response model and uncertainty propagation; occurrence probabilities need an eligible historical occurrence denominator. Without those objects, retain qualitative risk and UNKNOWN quantitative risk.

## Freeze requirements and unresolved evidence

For every numerical transformation retain its definition, cohort and exclusions, hierarchy, evidence/precision requirement, uncertainty or shrinkage law, UNKNOWN behavior, development-selection provenance, validation results and immutable artifact digest. Separate method definition from fitted constants. A literature citation may justify the estimator's assumptions; it cannot fill a missing policy value.

The same requirement applies to Selection Strength normalizations `C,W,L,Q,U,M,F,R`, constrained coefficients, research policy gates, confidence bands, reference disagreement boundaries and non-triviality margins. F13 predictions, baselines or replay identities do not themselves select these values. An independent reference needs a distinct declared methodological lineage and cutoff-valid validation evidence; an uncalibrated unavailable alternative cannot be manufactured from ensemble spread or a duplicate fit.

All evidence-dependent constants remain INCONCLUSIVE until an audited chronological corpus supplies raw predictions, exact outcome settlements, baseline/reference predictions, research states, adversarial records and later untouched validation/evaluation with enough cluster-aware precision. The contract must identify unsupported dimensions and refrain from emitting quantified support for them. RESEARCH_ONLY is a mode restriction; it does not authorize fabricated scores, silent gate relaxation, or production PLAY.
