## What the unchanged model needs

[HistoricalMatch](../../src/matchvet/t09.py) can represent full-time home/away goals independently of corners, cards, shots, or half-time goals. The [T11 training path](../../src/matchvet/t11.py), used by F13's full-time goal family, requires cutoff-valid completed matches, exact UTC kickoff, identified home/away teams, observed full-time integer goals, and retained source/assertion provenance. A date-only dataset cannot be converted by inventing a kickoff time. Source publication in 2019 does not establish a MatchVet observation in 2019. The real acquisition/observation must precede the prospective target cutoff.

The unchanged T11 defaults require at least three rows and an effective-history weight sum of at least three. Each row's recency weight is `2 ** (-age_days / 180)` and may also receive a regime discount. Three strictly earlier ordinary matches therefore have a sum below three. Four sufficiently recent rows are a numerical lower bound when no regime discount applies; they are not proof that a particular selected graph qualifies. Four rows within 74 days give a sum above three before regime discounts. Unseen target teams can use shared history if its row count meets the existing shared-history requirement. The model does not require a historical corpus from all seven prospective target leagues.

Manual observation of recent real results may thus be smaller than adopting an entire external dataset. It still needs lawful publication consultation, normalized fact retention, source provenance, exact UTC kickoff, and durable private backup/replay. It cannot relabel copied source facts as self-authored rights. LF05 itself only covers fixture observations and must not be expanded into a result/history authorization by this note.

## Smallest practical permission request

Prioritize the existing official manual-source grantors if the main closure review finds they can also supply recent completed results. Ask only for the extra history fields, not events, lineups, odds or video. If that is unavailable, Football Charts is a recent zero-cost alternative worth asking about. The explicit research access terms reduce the question, but do not answer it completely.

For each actual official publisher used by an eligible manual path, the narrow combined request is:

> May our named operator manually consult your official fixture and final-result publications and retain only competition/team identity, match identity, exact UTC kickoff, fixture status, and regulation-time home/away final goals, with publication URL, displayed publication/update time and our observation time? We need a bounded prospective Matchweek and a small recent historical corpus, followed by final-result settlement for the selected matches. Please confirm zero-cost private RESEARCH_ONLY statistical derivation, normalized fact/citation retention, and private backup, restore and offline replay. We will not scrape, automate publication access, copy pages/screenshots/video, redistribute a dataset or promote a commercial product. Please identify your authority to grant the rights, any required attribution, allowed scope and duration, and what withdrawal changes for future collection versus already-lawfully-retained evidence.

This combined question does not extend LF05 beyond its existing five manual competition policies, replace the actual provider attempts required by LF02, or create manual Spain/Portugal adapters. The parent closure review must preserve those path restrictions and identify the separate authorized current-adapter inputs where necessary.

Draft for `contact@football-charts.com`, identified on its [official terms page](https://www.football-charts.com/terms):

> MatchVet seeks a zero-cost, private RESEARCH_ONLY permission for a bounded prospective research graph. We need a small retained corpus of completed match IDs, competition identity, home/away team identity, exact UTC kickoff and regular-time final scores from your current/previous-season API. May we access these fields by the documented keyless API within your limits, keep exact responses privately where audit evidence requires them, normalize those facts, use them as internal derived statistical-model inputs, and preserve private backups plus offline restore/replay after access ends? We will retain your attribution and will not redistribute a dataset, sell data, or promote a commercial product. Please identify the upstream source/rightsholder for those fields, confirm that you can grant these exact operations, and specify any time limit, deletion rule, withdrawal effect or additional attribution. Do you provide exact UTC kickoff and unambiguous regulation-time scores for that retained corpus? Please confirm that no fee is required.

This is a draft only. No permission request was sent. StatsBomb/Impect would need equivalent retained-operation clarification against their named edit/reproduction restrictions; they are alternatives, not additional mandatory grantors. Do not ask every candidate for permission or add them all to the selected graph.

## Result

An open licence alone does not solve F13 chronology and weighted-history sufficiency. The strongest explicitly licensed releases reviewed here are too old or too small; the recent corpus candidates require exact permission and operational evidence. This does not prove a zero-cost lawful solution is impossible. The smallest viable statistical dependency may be a small recent rights-cleared manual corpus already covered by the prospective fixture/outcome publication grants. Otherwise seek one recent corpus grant, with actual exact kickoff/score evidence and unchanged model admissibility proven before any prospective collection.
