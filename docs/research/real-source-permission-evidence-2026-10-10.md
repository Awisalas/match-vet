## Exact evidence required before any prospective permission approval

The human decision authority must identify the actual operator/legal capacity and whether its research purpose meets provider definitions. For each selected source, obtain a current licence, applicable written rightsholder/provider answer, or a competent documented legal basis covering the exact access mode, competition/season/capability, minimal facts/citations versus copied content, internal research, separate raw/normalized retention, backups, restore and offline replay. Resolve third-party source lineage and any termination/deletion/withdrawal duties. Specifically resolve Football-Data automated research; each selected OpenFootball upstream; all seven actual official publishers used by LF02/LF05; each contextual publisher; and Open-Meteo's actual use classification and attribution.

The source decision must not promise immutable raw retention where a grant requires deletion. Historical identities/status decisions must not be rewritten, but new authorization, present reuse and copying must stop when rights are changed, withdrawn, expired or uncertain; retained bytes may be replayed only under a surviving lawful basis. None of this changes F01 completeness or converts missing facts into ABSENT. No source-authorization implementation issue should be opened on this evidence: first obtain the missing human/provider evidence for an actual graph.
