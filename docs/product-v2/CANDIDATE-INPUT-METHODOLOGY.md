## Frozen evidence and chronology contract

One decision names its exact F06 membership and controlling revision, F07 Match Evidence Cutoff, F10 requirement catalog, F11 evidence, F13 engine/model inputs and result, F14 Preference Profile, T15 Policy Version, and derivation dependencies. F09 health describes an acquisition capability and attempt; it does not establish evidence truth, research completion or a score.

Historical cases contain one canonical fixture, exact teams/role/competition/kickoff, evidence known at that case's cutoff, the raw prediction made without its result, training and calibration membership, rule-selection period, model/configuration digests, and subsequently observed exact settlement with source availability time. Validity requires prediction cutoff before the predicted kickoff, each prediction input available by that cutoff, no target outcome in fitting or selection, and outcome availability by the later derivation cutoff. Retrospective replay is admissible only from verifiable historical information states and an audited outer evaluation scheme. It must be labeled retrospective; publication after the event cannot prove a contemporaneous forecast.

Development uses nested rolling origins. At each origin, all fitting, baseline construction, preprocessing, calibration and parameter selection use preceding eligible evidence. Later validation selects a candidate profile. A later evaluation block never selects the profile it evaluates. Results reused for revision lose their unseen status. Period boundaries and reuse permissions are retained, not inferred from fixture date. The V2 per-match cutoff applies; V1's single Matchweek cutoff is not silently substituted. [Forecast-origin justification](https://otexts.com/fpp3/tscv.html).

Eligibility never depends on which outcome favors a candidate. Unknown timing, unknown required outcomes, target-result leakage, unresolved duplicate revisions, changed settlement contracts and corrupt sources produce an exclusion with a reason. The exact T10 family, backed side, line and phase determine settlement. Binary markets have structural PUSH probability zero; whole-goal Asian Handicaps retain WIN/PUSH/LOSS. VOID supplies no predictive scoring outcome. PUSH is not WIN and is never added to success or silently deleted to condition on non-PUSH.

Shared evidence is grouped by fixture, source origin and derived chain. Evaluation retains a complete fixture vector and Friday-to-Monday Matchweek/time-block identity. One fixture with 37 preference rows is one shared outcome unit. Temporal and shared-team dependence require declared uncertainty treatment; grouping by fixture alone does not prove independence.

## 2. Historical Reliability

Freeze descriptive settlement scores. For case `i` and its actual outcome set `K`, let `p_ik` be its frozen probability and `y_ik` the observed class indicator:

- Categorical Brier loss: `BS_i = sum(k in K) (p_ik - y_ik)^2`.
- Log loss: `LL_i = -log(p_i,observed)`. A realized zero probability yields infinity; no silent epsilon or clipping.
- Paired skill evidence: model loss minus the applicable cutoff-frozen baseline loss on the same case. Baselines are fitted earlier, never from the result they score.
- Full-vector calibration diagnostics: within predeclared prediction groups, observed class frequency minus mean predicted class probability. Groups and bins cannot be selected from evaluation outcomes.

These diagnostics use genuine out-of-sample model, calibration, uncertainty and baseline cases under the exact topology. They do not create a second selection score. Proper scoring rewards honest distributions; loss orientation and calibration are kept separate from gate acceptance. [Proper-score authority](https://sites.stat.washington.edu/people/raftery/Research/PDF/Gneiting2007jasa.pdf), [full-vector calibration](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v89/vaicenavicius19a.html).

**R1, normalized reliability and sample criterion: INCONCLUSIVE.** Required mapping is `R = f_R(score, calibration, coverage, paired baseline comparisons, interval precision, stability)` with applicable dimensions for the primary model, calibration, uncertainty method and baseline. No mapping, interval acceptance region or shrinkage parameter is selected. Use the settled hierarchy and supported ancestor; lack of evidence at every level leaves R UNKNOWN. Descriptive means require eligible cases; claims of reliability additionally require later paired comparisons with interval precision adequate for the frozen acceptance decision.

Unique fixture/Matchweek counts and weight concentration are retained separately. `n_weight = (sum w)^2 / sum(w^2)` describes unequal-weight concentration, not the effective sample after overlapping markets or temporal dependence. Any reported `effective_sample` needs a metric-specific dependence/precision derivation. Recency weights cannot be silently adopted from T09's team-feature rules. Raw diagnostics are not automatically mapped through `1 - error`. Protected reliability identity includes all four dependent model/method identities, paired cohorts, fold definitions, uncertainty configuration and validation. No such fitted record is retained in the inspected stores.

## 3. Historical Baseline and Non-Triviality

Freeze the hierarchy and exact topology. Each narrower role, league or supported context borrows from the applicable family/exact-line parent only under a frozen eligibility rule. Missing local evidence permits a validated supported ancestor, not a made-up root or pooling unrelated market base rates. Record period, cohort, effective sample, uncertainty, shrinkage level, recency/regime treatment and source/version digests. Unknown outcome counts are not empirical zeros. T09 goal/corner means and T14's default empirical diagnostic reference are not these baseline artifacts.

**B1, baseline estimator/profile: INCONCLUSIVE.** A justified candidate is a binary Beta or topology-correct categorical Dirichlet model. For known category counts `n_gk` and positive concentration `a_gk`, its candidate posterior mean is `(n_gk + a_gk) / (N_g + sum a_gk)`. Binary topology has only WIN/LOSS and no PUSH prior mass. A parent-centered prior could use `a_gk = lambda_g * b_parent,k`; neither lambda nor root prior is selected here. Parent/child observations cannot be treated twice as independent evidence. Fractional recency weighting changes the likelihood and cannot borrow unweighted conjugate variance unchanged. The estimator remains a candidate until selected and validated. [Dirichlet density](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.stats.dirichlet.html), [Beta quantiles](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.stats.beta.html).

Eligible data are outcome-complete, cutoff-available exact settlements in a prespecified baseline cohort. Baseline prediction at every evaluation origin uses only earlier evidence. Minimum evidence is identified population/parent structure, later rolling-origin comparisons and clustered precision sufficient for the applicable gate. Confidence level, concentration fitting, population window, regime response and specialization criterion remain unselected. Conditional conjugate intervals do not establish validity under nonstationarity or clustering; both must be examined on genuine chronological evidence. With no supported ancestor, baseline, G4 status and baseline-dependent components remain UNKNOWN.

**B2, structural triviality and G4 lift: INCONCLUSIVE.** Preserve #13's joint requirement for an uncertainty-adjusted near-automatic baseline WIN rate and insufficient meaningful match-specific discrimination. The numerical boundary, discrimination/resolution statistic, acceptance margin and G4 lift definition are not selected. The existing rank component `max(0, CP_WIN - b_WIN)` is authoritative for ranking W; it does not choose the separate G4 supplied lift or prove structural triviality. Eligible data need baseline and match-specific predictions generated before each outcome, including sufficiently precise supported line/context groups. The artifact records both conditions and reasons; prohibited contracts never enter evaluation. Hand-authored `non_trivial=True` or `structural_trivial=False` cannot bypass missing methodology.

### G6 tail risk and coverage evidence

Freeze three separate estimands: probability-interval coverage targets the unobserved conditional settlement probability; predictive coverage targets an observed outcome region; calibration compares probabilities with outcome frequencies over comparable predictions. Testing whether the observed WIN indicator lies inside F13's probability interval cannot certify probability coverage. A simulation with known probabilities can examine model-conditional interval behavior, but cannot create genuine historical evidence. Real outcome calibration/coverage diagnostics cannot silently be relabeled latent probability coverage. [Coverage definitions](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2020/file/26d88423fc6da243ffddf161ca712757-Paper.pdf).

**U1, tail-risk definition: INCONCLUSIVE.** The existing consumer accepts one bounded scalar but does not define its event/estimand. A count-tail event has a different meaning from uncertainty that the WIN probability is below a gate. A future rule must choose and justify the relevant estimand, supported family/line event, available uncertainty law, calibration evidence and acceptance limit. An interval quantile level, LOSS UCB, missing-mass estimate or uncertainty multiplier is not substituted automatically. Minimum evidence is a frozen measurable event, exact predictions and outcome/probability validation appropriate to that event, with adequate tail precision. Parent fallback must preserve that estimand; absent support leaves tail risk UNKNOWN.

**U2, empirical reliability/coverage profile: INCONCLUSIVE.** Specify the target, model assumptions, validation procedure, confidence level, acceptable miscoverage/precision, subgroup fallback and misspecification checks. Genuine later full-vector calibration and tail diagnostics are required, alongside explicitly labeled conditional interval diagnostics where justified. An uncertainty/error relationship must account for outcome randomness and task difficulty; it is not itself coverage. The current #13 wording does not identify an observable per-fixture latent probability coverage criterion from one outcome. That decision needs clarification and a versioned measurable protocol, not simply more result rows.

A protected uncertainty validation artifact declares exactly which claim is supported and its cohort/folds and assumptions. ### Paired Statistical Tie evidence

Freeze the target as the current gate-qualified pair's strength difference. A candidate construction is: for the current match candidates `a,b`, `D_r = T15_strength(a; Z_r) - T15_strength(b; Z_r)`, with the same coherent historical block/parameter/calibration/baseline/scenario draw `Z_r` applied to both. The resulting interval contains zero for a Statistical Tie under #13. A historical mean performance difference is a validation diagnostic, not this current-candidate latent strength difference. The existing ordered tie breakers remain lower uncertainty, stronger Data Quality, lower Failure Risk, stronger Historical Reliability, then preference order only when still indistinguishable.

**T1, joint uncertainty profile: INCONCLUSIVE.** The construction also requires a declared conditioning/selection law: whether eligibility stays fixed to the observed qualified pair, how gate-crossing replicates affect the estimand, and what happens when a replicate has invalid or UNKNOWN components. These choices are not selected. Dropping such draws or silently conditioning on survival changes the law and cannot be a default. D cannot be certified until that law is defined and validated. Eligible support comprises paired predictions/strength inputs and overlapping-market outcomes across genuine chronological blocks. A supported shared pair population must preserve covariance and topology; independent marginal intervals or a family mean cannot replace it. Minimum evidence is coherent joint draws and later ranking/paired validation with adequate interval and simulation precision. Confidence level, block selection law/length, interval construction, replication tolerance and joint scenario law remain unselected. Chronological blocks preserve dependence under declared assumptions, not under every structural break. [Dependent block selection research](https://www.math.ucsd.edu/~politis/SBblock-revER.pdf).

T1 also needs a coherent rule for three or more finalists. Pairwise intervals containing zero need not create a transitive tie relation. For example, in a hypothetical strength order A > B > C, ties A/B and B/C but not A/C can conflict with reversed tie-break preferences and create a comparator cycle. A pair artifact alone cannot certify a deterministic justified total order. Freeze the compatible multi-finalist comparison/tie-group and simultaneous uncertainty rule without changing T15's strength formula or ordered tie breakers. This design decision is unresolved in addition to its evidence-dependent calibration.

The protected tie artifact names the unordered preference pair, oriented D definition, shared draw/cohort digests, interval, joint law and validation. Insufficient support leaves distinguishability UNKNOWN and blocks a justified primary among the affected finalists. Current T15 tests marginal interval overlap and scalar tolerance; its candidate fields cannot encode the required pair/covariance contract. A focused compatible T15 comparison seam is needed before readiness. It must consume paired evidence and retain the existing score and tie-break order, not create another ranking system.
